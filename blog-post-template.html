5 Breathing Techniques to Calm Your Mind Before Class

Before you even step onto the mat, your breath can set the tone for your entire practice. Here are five simple techniques you can use anywhere — at your desk, in the car, or right before class begins.

1. Box Breathing

Inhale for four counts, hold for four, exhale for four, hold for four. Repeat for one to two minutes to steady a racing mind.

2. Extended Exhale

Make your exhale longer than your inhale — try inhaling for four counts and exhaling for six. This activates the parasympathetic nervous system and signals safety to the body.
