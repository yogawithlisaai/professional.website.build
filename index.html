Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find yoga classes in Berkeley, CA?

Yoga with Lisa offers yoga classes in Berkeley, CA, including weekly Vinyasa Flow classes designed for beginners and experienced practitioners. Classes focus on building strength, improving flexibility, reducing stress, and creating a consistent practice in a welcoming community.

Are your Vinyasa yoga classes in Berkeley suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our Vinyasa yoga classes in Berkeley are beginner-friendly while offering options for intermediate and advanced students. Every class includes modifications, allowing you to practice safely at your own pace.

Do you offer private yoga sessions in Berkeley?

Yes. We provide private yoga sessions in Berkeley for individuals, couples, and small groups. Private sessions are personalized to help you improve flexibility, recover from stress, build strength, or deepen your yoga practice.

Do you teach corporate yoga in the Bay Area?

Yes. Yoga with Lisa offers corporate yoga programs throughout the Bay Area for businesses, universities, and organizations. Corporate wellness sessions can include yoga, mindfulness, meditation, breathwork, and stress-management practices tailored to your team's needs.

I'm searching for yoga near me in Berkeley. Why choose Yoga with Lisa?

If you're looking for yoga near me in Berkeley, Yoga with Lisa combines science-backed instruction with an inclusive teaching style. Founded by Dr. Lisa Eshun-Wilson, who holds a PhD from UC Berkeley, classes emphasize sustainable movement, stress reduction, and long-term wellness.

Do you offer yoga classes near Oakland, CA?

Yes. Students from Oakland, CA, and surrounding East Bay communities regularly attend our classes. Our convenient Berkeley location makes it easy for anyone looking for quality yoga instruction in the Bay Area.

What makes Dr. Lisa different from other yoga instructors in Berkeley?

Dr. Lisa Eshun-Wilson is a yoga instructor in Berkeley with a PhD in Molecular and Cell Biology from UC Berkeley. She combines scientific knowledge with years of teaching experience to create evidence-informed yoga classes focused on mobility, stress relief, and overall wellbeing.

Do you offer sound bath yoga in Berkeley?

Yes. Throughout the year, Yoga with Lisa hosts sound bath yoga experiences in Berkeley, along with meditation, breathwork, and special wellness events designed to promote relaxation and nervous system recovery.

What is a yoga retreat?

A yoga retreat is a multi-day immersive experience that combines daily yoga practice with rest, community, and time away from routine. Retreats typically include guided classes, meditation and breathwork sessions, nourishing meals, and unstructured time to recover. Unlike a weekly class, a retreat gives you several uninterrupted days to deepen your practice and reset your nervous system.

Does Yoga with Lisa host yoga retreats in Bali?

Yes. Yoga with Lisa hosts an annual international yoga retreat in Bali, combining daily vinyasa practice, meditation and breathwork, and time to explore the island. Spots are limited and typically fill well in advance. Secure your spot today.

How do I join the next Yoga with Lisa retreat?

Join the waitlist to be notified first when registration opens, since retreat spots are released to the waitlist before they are announced publicly. You can also reach out at contact@yogawithlisa.ai with questions about pricing, accommodations, travel, or whether a retreat is right for your level.

Can I practice online if I can't attend classes in Berkeley?

Absolutely. In addition to in-person classes, Yoga with Lisa offers on-demand yoga, guided meditations, and wellness programs that you can access from anywhere, making it easy to maintain a consistent practice.

How do I get started with Yoga with Lisa?

Getting started is simple. You can book an in-person yoga class, schedule a private session, join the membership for on-demand classes, or reserve your spot on one of our wellness retreats. Whether you're new to yoga or an experienced practitioner, there's an option designed for your goals.
